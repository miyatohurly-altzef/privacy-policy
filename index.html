NewsTape Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

In short: NewsTape has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. The developer does not collect, see, sell or share any of your data. Your settings stay in your browser.

What NewsTape is

NewsTape is a browser extension that shows a scrolling bar of news headlines and market prices at the top of the web pages you visit.

Data the developer collects

None. NewsTape does not send anything to the developer. There is no tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting and no user account.

Data stored on your device

NewsTape saves the following in your browser's own extension storage:

  • Your settings: topics, countries, feeds and accounts you follow, assets you track, theme, language and similar choices.
  • Amounts you enter as holdings, and wallet addresses you choose to follow.
  • If you follow a Slack channel, the Slack token you enter. It is stored only on this device: it is never synced, exported or sent anywhere except to Slack.
  • A cache of recently fetched headlines and prices, and which headlines were already shown, so every tab shows the same bar without downloading it again.

If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your browser may sync your settings across your own devices. That sync is run by your browser's maker (Google or Mozilla), not by NewsTape. Holdings are left out of settings you export or share.

Requests to third-party services

To show what you asked for, the extension fetches public data directly from the services that provide it. Like any web request, these services can see your IP address and what was requested. Only the information needed for each request is sent:

  • News: your chosen topics, search terms and countries go to Google News to get headlines. Feeds and accounts you follow are fetched from their own sites (for example YouTube, Reddit, GitHub, Bluesky, Mastodon, Medium, Substack or any RSS feed you add). If you follow a Slack channel, your Slack token is sent only to Slack, to read that channel.
  • Prices: the tickers, coins and collections you track go to public market services such as Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, Yahoo Finance, CoinGecko, DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, pump.fun, OpenSea, Magic Eden and Manifold.
  • Wallets: wallet addresses you choose to follow go to public blockchain explorers (mempool.space, Blockscout and the public Solana network) to read their activity.
  • Sports: teams and leagues you follow are fetched from public sports score services.
  • Translation: only if you turn on "Translate headlines", headline text is sent to Google Translate to be translated into your language.
  • Images and icons: news site addresses go to the DuckDuckGo icon service to show site logos, and headline pictures are loaded from the sites that host them.

Each of these services handles requests under its own privacy policy.

The web pages you visit

NewsTape runs on every page so it can show the bar there. On those pages it only reads the page's colors and font (to match the bar's look, if you use the "Adaptive" theme) and the position of the page's fixed headers (to move them below the bar). It does not read, store or send the content of the pages you visit, your browsing history, or anything you type.

Notifications

If you turn on desktop notifications (for example for wallet activity or game scores), the browser asks for permission first. Notifications are created on your device and nothing is sent anywhere to show them.

Links you open

Clicking a headline, price or the "Donate" item opens that website in a new tab. That site's own privacy policy applies from then on.

Selling and sharing

NewsTape does not sell your data, does not transfer it to third parties for advertising or any other purpose, and does not use it to decide creditworthiness or for lending.

Children

NewsTape is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes everything it stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
